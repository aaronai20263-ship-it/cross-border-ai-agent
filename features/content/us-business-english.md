# 地道美式商务英语表达（开发信与话术优化）

本文件提供面向美国市场的开发信、异议应对、短语替换及语气自查标准。所有内容禁止中式英语（Chinglish）。

## 一、美式商务写作核心原则（第一性原理拆解）

**基本事实**：美国商务人士每天收100-200封邮件，90%在3秒内删除。

**结论**：活下来的邮件必须同时满足——
1. 第一句让对方觉得“这跟我有关”（提到具体公司信号，不是“I hope this email finds you well”）；
2. 全文不超过100词；
3. 行动号召（CTA）让对方用5个字以内就能回答。

**语气标准**：像你在咖啡店跟同行聊天，不是坐在会议室念PPT。

## 二、开发信模板（直接替换可用）

**Subject**: Quick question about [Company]'s [specific market/region]

**Body**:
Hi [First Name],

I noticed [Company] recently [specific signal — e.g., expanded into Germany, hired a US sales lead, launched a new SKU].

Most brands at that stage run into [your target problem — e.g., high CAC, low AOV, localization friction].

We help [similar companies] achieve [specific result]. Example: [one short client win, e.g., "boosted UK conversion by 22% in 6 weeks"].

Worth 15 minutes to walk through a few quick ideas for [Company]? I'm flexible this week.

Best,
[Your First Name]
[Title] | [Company]
[LinkedIn URL]

## 三、必改短语替换表（彻底告别中式英语）

| 中式/过时表达（禁用） | 地道美式（改用） |
|---------------------|------------------|
| I am writing to inform you... | I'm reaching out regarding... |
| Please allow me to introduce... | Quick intro — I'm [name] from... |
| I was just wondering if perhaps... | Quick question — do you still...? |
| As per my previous email... | Following up on my note from [day]... |
| I would like to follow up... | I wanted to follow up on... |
| Yours sincerely / Yours faithfully | Best / Best regards |
| Dear Sir/Madam | Hi [First Name] / Hello [Team] |
| We are committed to providing... | We help with... |
| Please do not hesitate to contact | Feel free to reach out |
| In order to... | To... |

## 四、异议应对库（美式口语化版）

**异议：价格太高**
> Totally get it — budget's always a consideration. What we usually see is ROI kicking in within [X] weeks on a small test. Happy to start with a minimal pilot so you can see the numbers before scaling.

**异议：已有供应商**
> That makes sense. Curious — what metrics matter most to you right now? We could run a quick side-by-side comparison to see if there's incremental value. No pressure either way.

**异议：没预算 / 明年再说**
> Completely understand — budget cycles are real. Would a free 30-min diagnostic be useful? We'd map out potential ROI and payback period, so when budget does open up, you've got data ready.

**异议：我们不感兴趣**
> Fair enough. Out of curiosity — what's the main blocker? Timing, product fit, or something else? If it's fit, I'll stop pitching; if it's timing, I'll check back later.

## 五、美式邮件发送前自查清单

- [ ] 邮件总字数 ≤ 100词？
- [ ] 第一句提到了对方公司的具体信号（非“你好”）？
- [ ] 去掉了 all 过度正式开场（I am writing to / please allow me）？
- [ ] CTA是一个对方用5个字能回答的问题？
- [ ] 去掉了空洞形容词（革命性、最佳、强大）？
- [ ] 大声读一遍——听起来像你在说话，还是像机器翻译？
